## Why This Matters
KSH purchased dialysis equipment. It has been completely idle for 12 consecutive months. This is foregone revenue from a capital asset that is already paid for — every month of inactivity is pure opportunity cost. The fix is a referral pipeline, not capital investment.

## Key Findings (Inv 4)

- Session history: March 2025 (2 sessions) and April 2025 (1 session) — only 3 sessions ever recorded
- Zero sessions recorded at any point before March 2025 either — the programme never fully launched
- Revenue potential: KES 52K–140K/month based on session volume × billing rate estimates
- Root cause: no referral pipeline connecting nephrology/internal medicine specialists to KSH's equipment
- TENRI: dialysis workflow shifted from inpatient to same-day between 2018–2022 — a successful precedent for building a dialysis programme at a private hospital

---

## Recommended Actions
- Targeted outreach to **3–5 Kisumu nephrology and internal medicine specialists** — inform them KSH has dialysis capacity available, ask for patient referrals
- Draft a referral agreement / SLA with at least one nephrologist as anchor
- Identify 5–10 existing KSH patients with chronic kidney disease (from evaluation visits) as initial pipeline
- No procurement, no hiring, no capital spend required — this is a business development action, 1–2 weeks to first result
- If programme launches: track session volume monthly against the KES 52K–140K/month target
